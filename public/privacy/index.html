Privacy, by design

Effective: August 27, 2026. Pocket Sprite Pack processes your artwork entirely in your browser. Files are not uploaded to us.

Data on your device

The app stores your last project in IndexedDB so you can recover it after a refresh, and stores a license token and verification date in localStorage if you unlock Pro. You can clear the project from the app and clear all data using your browser settings.

License verification

When restoring or checking Pro, the license token is sent to the Sociobot billing API. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record and handles payment information under its own policies. The packer does not receive card details.

Network and analytics

There are no advertising trackers, third-party fonts, or behavioral analytics. The hosting platform may keep ordinary security and aggregate request logs. The app contacts the network only to load its own files, update its offline cache, and verify a license.

Contact

Questions may be sent through the project support channel linked from the product listing. Do not include artwork or license tokens in a public report.
